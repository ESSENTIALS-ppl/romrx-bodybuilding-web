Full Body vs. Upper/Lower Split: Which Builds More Muscle?

Lifters argue about this constantly. Should you train your whole body each session, or split it into upper and lower days? The honest answer from the science may surprise you. This guide shows what actually drives growth.

The Short Answer

When your total weekly sets are the same, both build muscle equally well. A 2024 review compared split and full-body routines and found no meaningful difference in muscle growth or strength when volume was matched (Ramos-Campo et al., 2024, Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research).

That review pooled 14 studies and found the same result for arm size, leg size, and total lean mass (Fit AF Nutrition summary of the meta-analysis). So the split itself is not magic. Volume and frequency are what matter.

When to Choose Full Body

  • You train 2 to 3 days per week.
  • You want every muscle hit often.
  • You are short on time and want efficient sessions.

When to Choose Upper/Lower

  • You train 4 days per week.
  • You want more exercises per muscle each session.
  • You like more focused, higher-volume days.

Both can hit each muscle twice a week, which is the frequency the research supports (Verro Training review of frequency research).

Where Range of Motion Fits In

Your split should serve your range of motion, not fight it. Exercises with a deep, loaded stretch drive extra growth (The Strength Equation on stretch-mediated hypertrophy). An upper/lower split gives you room to include more full-range variations per muscle, while a full-body split keeps frequency high so each muscle gets worked through its full length more often.

Key Takeaways

  • At equal volume, full body and upper/lower build muscle the same.
  • Full body fits 2 to 3 days per week; upper/lower fits 4 days.
  • What matters most is weekly volume and hitting each muscle 2+ times.
  • Pick the split you will actually stick to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is full body or a split better for building muscle?

Neither is better when total weekly volume is equal. Both build muscle the same.

How many days should I train on each?

Full body works well at 2 to 3 days per week. Upper/lower fits 4 days per week.

Does a split let me lift heavier?

It lets you do more focused work per muscle each session, but total weekly volume is still what drives growth.

Can beginners do an upper/lower split?

Yes, but many beginners do well on full body since it hits each muscle often and keeps sessions simple.
